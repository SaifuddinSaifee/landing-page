# EliteClass Silicon Valley Excellence Scholarship Programs

## Performance Excellence Scholarship

### Overview
The Performance Excellence Scholarship rewards outstanding academic achievement in certification exams during the program. This scholarship is designed for students who demonstrate exceptional understanding and mastery of the technical content.

### Benefits
- Full reimbursement of program fee based on your package:
  * Premium Package: $2,499
  * Advanced Package: $3,499
  * Expert Package: $4,499
- Recognition in the EliteClass Alumni Network
- Special mention during program ceremonies
- Priority access to networking events
- Featured profile on EliteClass success stories
- Scholarship discount for future programs
- Extended one-on-one mentoring sessions
- Special access to future EliteClass programs

### Eligibility Requirements
- Score 90% or above in all six certification exams mentioned in the "Certifications Covered" section
- 100% attendance in all sessions
- Active participation in all program components
- Professional conduct throughout the program
- Positive feedback from trainers and mentors
  
### Certifications Covered
1. AZ-900: Microsoft Azure Fundamentals
2. AI-900: Microsoft Azure AI Fundamentals
3. MS-900: Microsoft 365 Fundamentals
4. DP-900: Microsoft Azure Data Fundamentals
5. SC-900: Microsoft Security Fundamentals
6. PL-900: Microsoft Power Platform Fundamentals

### Terms and Conditions
1. All six certifications must be completed within your program duration:
   * Premium Package: 2 weeks
   * Advanced Package: 3 weeks
   * Expert Package: 4 weeks
2. Scholarship is non-transferable
3. Maintaining professional conduct is mandatory
4. Regular attendance and participation required
5. All decisions are final and binding by the EliteClass scholarship committee
6. Vouchers of the additional certifications must be purchased from EliteClass, and will only be reimbursed if the student has completed all six certifications within their program duration.

## Multi-Certification Achievement Scholarship

### Overview
The Multi-Certification Achievement Scholarship recognizes and rewards students who successfully complete all six Microsoft certifications, regardless of their chosen package, demonstrating exceptional dedication and technical capability.

### Benefits
- Full reimbursement for all additional certification costs (vouchers needed to complete six certifications)
- Recognition in the EliteClass Alumni Network

### Certifications Required
Must complete a total of six certifications, including:
- All certifications included in your chosen package
- Additional certifications from the following options:
  * Microsoft Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900)
  * Microsoft Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-900)
  * Microsoft 365 Fundamentals (MS-900)
  * Azure Data Fundamentals (DP-900)
  * Microsoft Security Fundamentals (SC-900)
  * Power Platform Fundamentals (PL-900)

### Eligibility Requirements
- Successfully complete all six Microsoft certifications with passing score
- 100% attendance in all sessions
- Active participation in program components
- Professional conduct throughout the program
- Positive contribution to program community

### Terms and Conditions
1. All certifications must be completed during the program duration
2. Benefits must be utilized within two weeks of program start date.
3. Scholarship is non-transferable
4. Regular progress updates required
5. All decisions are final and binding by the EliteClass scholarship committee
6. Vouchers of the additional certifications must be purchased from EliteClass, and will only be reimbursed if the student has completed all six certifications.

## Elite 50 Referral Excellence Program

### Overview
The Elite 50 Program is a prestigious referral initiative that rewards exceptional community building and leadership. This program offers substantial benefits for those who successfully refer 50 students to EliteClass within two years of completing their own program. Participants must choose between this program and the standard referral program (which offers ₹2,500 Amazon vouchers per referral) at the time of their first referral.

### Benefits

#### Financial Rewards
- Cash scholarship of 2 Lakh INR upon reaching 50 successful referrals
- Note: Elite 50 participants are not eligible for the standard ₹2,500 Amazon voucher per referral

#### Career Advancement
- Guaranteed paid internship at EliteClass
  * Duration: 3 months
  * Role: Program Coordinator / Technical Trainer
  * Location: Remote
  * Stipend: As per company policy
  * Possibility of full-time conversion based on performance

#### Higher Education Support
- Personalized Letters of Recommendation (LOR) for master's programs
  * Direct communication to universities through LORs
  * Customized LORs highlighting specific achievements
  * Unlimited number of LORs for different universities
  * Special mention of Elite 50 Program achievement
  * Detailed description of leadership and community building skills

#### Additional Benefits
- Recognition as Elite 50 Member in Alumni Network
- Special mention on company website and social media
- Priority access to future EliteClass programs and events
- Exclusive networking opportunities with Silicon Valley professionals
- Mentorship opportunities for future batches

### Eligibility Requirements
- Successfully refer 50 students within two calendar years after program completion
- Referred students must complete the program successfully
- Maintain professional communication and conduct throughout
- Must have completed the EliteClass program with 100% attendance

### Program Terms
1. The 24-month referral period starts from the date of first successful referral
2. A referral is considered successful only after the referred student completes the program
3. Internship opportunity must be utilized within 18 months of achieving Elite 50 status
4. LORs will be provided for up to 3 years after achieving Elite 50 status
5. All benefits are subject to maintaining professional conduct and program standards
6. All referred students (referees) receive a ₹5,000 Amazon voucher regardless of which referral program the referrer chooses
7. Once enrolled in the Elite 50 program, participants cannot switch to the standard referral program
